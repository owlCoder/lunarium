# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a respectful, welcoming environment for contributors.

Be constructive and considerate. Do not engage in harassment, discrimination, bullying, or publishing another person's private information. Assume good intent, but accept feedback about impact. Maintain respectful technical discussions and critique ideas rather than people.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove disruptive content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

To report conduct concerns, contact repository maintainers privately via their GitHub profiles. Avoid filing public issues containing sensitive information.
